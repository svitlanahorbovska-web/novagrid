Who Owns Your Avatar?

Digital identity is becoming more valuable - and more complex.

As avatars accumulate history, style and social capital, a harder question follows close behind: who actually owns them? The platform that hosts the account? The player who built the identity over years? Somewhere in between, unresolved?

This isn’t an abstract debate. Bans, platform shutdowns and account transfers have already erased digital identities people spent years building - sometimes with real financial and emotional cost attached. As virtual identity becomes more central to how people present themselves, the lack of clear ownership starts to feel less like a technicality and more like a gap that needs closing.

Expect this conversation to get louder, not quieter, as more of daily life - social, creative, even professional - moves into spaces built and owned by someone else.
